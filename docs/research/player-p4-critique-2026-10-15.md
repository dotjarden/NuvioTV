## P1

**C1 (P1) The B17 rebuild loads from t=0 and drags the remux back to the start.** A new `AVPlayerItem` with no start position loads at 0 before the spec's seek can run. Segments behind the playhead are often absent: the remux always starts at segment 1 (`pollForFirstSegment` waits for `seg-00001`) and a resume seek repositions it, so segments 2…k of a resumed session were never written; a track switched mid-play has no `aud-T-00001`. `serveSegmentJIT` answers a missing file outside the producing window with `requestReposition(idx)` (`LocalHLSServer.swift:648-656`), then blocks 5 s and 503s. The rebuild would move production to the top of the file and likely end in "item failed" → mpv fallback. **Fix (applied, §5.3/§5.4):** `LocalHLSServer.setStartOffset(_:)` renders `#EXT-X-START:TIME-OFFSET=<pos>,PRECISE=YES` into the master for the rebuild only (nil = byte-identical); B proves on the headless rig that the first request after the swap is the playhead's segment with no reposition; fallback is a pre-ready `item.seek`; if neither works, B17 ships publish-only. Unit test and device step 4 oracle added.

**C2 (P1) The rebuild reverts the audio track before restoring it.** `audioRenditions` is a `let` built with `isDefault: track.selected` at `beginPlayback` (`NativePlaybackCoordinator.swift:548-557`), and the player's audible criteria re-apply to a new item. The new item therefore picks the original or preferred track, and the media-selection notification and the 3 s tick (`:785-786`) relay that pick through `syncAudioSelection` → `remux.selectAudio` (`:862-871`) before the spec's restore by name. The worker switches twice, with JIT blocks on files that will never be written. **Fix (applied):** the master renders audio `DEFAULT` from `selectedAudio()`; the rebuild clears both media-selection criteria on the player first (they are "once per item at setup" anyway, `:318-321`); `syncAudioSelection` returns early while `isRebuildingItem`; restore audio, then subtitles, then clear the flag.

## P2

**C3 Rebuild failure paths and observer inventory.** A pre-ready failure of the rebuilt item takes the `!readied` branch into `handlePrePlaybackItemFailure` (`:721-723`), the signaling retry, not the fallback the spec names; and the not-ready branch only logs every 10 s (`:741-748`), so a stuck rebuild freezes the picture. A rebuild failure classified `.unknown` would also be remembered as a bad link. **Fix (applied):** `rebuild != nil` routes `.failed` and 12 s without ready to `fallbackMidPlay`; new non-remembered `PlaybackFailureKind.local`. The spec now lists every observer: per item `observeTask`, `mediaSelectionObserver`, group caches, the two subtitle-delay tasks, the artwork attach; per player `timeControlObserver` and the criteria. There is no periodic time observer and no status KVO (status is polled).

**C4 Chapter origin.** The native timeline's zero is the first video keyframe: `originTicks = boundaryTicks[0]` (`RemuxSession.swift:552`), tfdt absolute (`:506-511`, `:586-587`), the same origin the subtitle sinks use (`:523-525`). `chapterSec − start_time` is wrong whenever the first keyframe is not at the container start. **Fix (applied):** `seconds(start:tbNum:tbDen:originSec:)` with `originSec = boundaryTicks[0] × videoTB`, read after the map exists (`:499`), stored with `_segmentMap`; the rig check verifies instead of discovering; tests updated. The native and mpv lists differ by `originSec − start_time` by design.

**C5 Pause card.** (a) `barVisible` started false, so a delegate that never fires on tvOS 26 would put the card over a visible bar. (b) `onAnyPress` from `pressesBegan` only ever sees presses AVPVC did not consume (in practice Menu with the bar hidden, `PlayerPanelHost.swift:72-84`). (c) "Hidden for the rest of that pause, the mpv rule" is not the mpv rule: `pauseCardVisible` (`MPVPlayerView.swift:3888-3890`) has no latch. **Fix (applied):** the card needs a `visible=false` callback during this pause (unknown = visible); presses come from a simultaneous all-press-types tap recogniser like `downPress`; no latch; hidden during a B17 rebuild. Fallback if the bar never hides while paused: drop the card on native (`NativePauseCardRule.supported = false`), keep "(mpv player)" in the copy, note it, and log a `contentOverlayView` card (drawn under AVKit's chrome) as the follow-up. The `bar visible=` log is kept in every build, transitions only.
