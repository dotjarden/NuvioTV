## P1 performance plan (ordered, with payoff/risk and who does it)

What the numbers say, mapped to code. Stage's per-press cost that Classic does not pay: the strip's `.mask` over the whole ScrollView (`Screens/Home/StripPager.swift:386-388`, `StripEdgeMask` `Screens/Home/StageView.swift:295-306`), the per-page `.visualEffect` opacity (`StripPager.swift:452-456`, 0.6 on the peeking page, `StripGeometry.swift:230-233`), the pager body re-running on every `positionId` write (`@State` at `StripPager.swift:292`, bound at 376), the stage swap and wash. Shared costs that Stage pays more often because it mounts a fresh row per press: `CachedImageLoader` publishes, the letterbox scan, URL family rebuilding, Kotlin string bridging in `CatalogRowView`. Order below is payoff over risk. Measure each step with the Wave 0 protocol (`walk-home.sh`, `hitches.py`) on Test 2 in Steven's config; keep a step only if the walk improves.
